The fine print

Privacy

Short version: we keep only what the app needs to work, and we never sell, share or track it.

What we store

  • Your account: your username and a salted, one-way scrypt hash of your password. We can't see your password.
  • Your tasks: titles, notes, checklists, dates and the other details you enter, plus trophy and streak progress.
  • Sessions: a hashed session token so you stay logged in for up to 30 days.

Cookies & tracking

One cookie, grimoire_session, keeps you signed in. It's HttpOnly and never used for anything else. There are no ads, analytics or third-party trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Your browser also keeps a local copy of your tasks so the app works offline. Logging out clears it.

Sharing

Nothing is public unless you turn on the share poster. It shows task titles, dates and dread levels only — never notes or checklists — and turning it off kills the link immediately. Your calendar-feed link is private; you can replace it any time.

Your control

Export everything as JSON from the app footer at any time. Deleting your account (user menu → Delete account) permanently removes your tasks, graveyard, trophies and sessions.
